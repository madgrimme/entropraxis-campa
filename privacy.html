Compa Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Compa ("the Service", "the Application") handles your information. Our philosophy is simple: your data is your own.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The Compa application does not have its own servers and does not collect, transmit, or store any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) about its users. We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics services.

2. How We Use Google User Data

Compa uses Google Sign-In for two specific purposes:

  1. To Authenticate You: To verify your identity as a unique user.
  2. To Store Your Data in Your Own Drive: To save your personal learning profile (a file named compa_profile.json) securely in your own Google Drive.

The application requests access using the `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file` scope. This is a highly restrictive permission. It means:

  • The application can **only access files that it has created itself**.
  • The application **cannot see, read, modify, or delete any of your other files** or documents in your Google Drive.
  • The creators of Compa have **no access** to your Google account or any of your files, including the profile created by the app.

3. Data Storage and Retention

All data generated by your use of the Service is stored in two places that you control:

  • Local Browser Storage: For fast, offline access. This data can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's cache.
  • Your Google Drive: As a single file that you own and can delete at any time.

We do not have a data retention policy because we do not have your data.

4. Children's Privacy

Compa is designed for users of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. All user data is handled as described above, residing entirely within the user's own device and Google account.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer at the email address provided on the Google consent screen.
