Compa Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Compa ("the Service", "the Application", "we") is a free learning companion. This policy explains what information Compa handles, where it lives, who can see it, and how you control it. The short version: your learning data belongs to you, is stored in your own Google account, and is never seen by the developer of Compa.

1. Information Compa does not collect

Compa has no servers of its own. It does not collect, transmit, or store personally identifiable information about its users. It does not use cookies, tracking pixels, advertising identifiers, or analytics services. The developer of Compa cannot see who uses the Service or how they use it.

2. Google user data Compa accesses

Compa uses Google Sign-In and requests a single Google Drive permission, the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. Through this scope Compa accesses:

  • Your basic Google account identity (name, email address, and profile picture), used only to sign you in and to label your session on your own device.
  • One file that Compa creates in your Google Drive, named compa_profile.json, containing your learning profile: the lessons you have completed, your responses, and the progress indicators Compa uses to choose your next lesson.

The drive.file scope is deliberately narrow. Compa can only open files it created itself. It cannot see, read, change, or delete any other file or folder in your Google Drive.

3. How Compa uses this data

Compa uses your Google user data for exactly two purposes:

  • To authenticate you, so that your learning profile is associated with your own Google account and no one else's.
  • To save your learning profile to, and read it back from, your own Google Drive, so that your progress follows you across devices and is not lost if your browser data is cleared.

Compa does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, market research, or any purpose other than providing the Service to you.

4. How lessons are generated

Compa generates personalised lessons using Google's Gemini API. To do this, the Application sends the relevant lesson content and the parts of your learning profile needed to personalise the lesson (for example, which skills you have practised and where you are struggling) directly from your device to the Gemini API. This transfer happens between your browser and Google; it does not pass through any server operated by the developer of Compa. Compa does not retain any of this data after the lesson has been generated. Google's handling of data sent to the Gemini API is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy.

5. Sharing and transfer of data

We do not share, sell, rent, or transfer Google user data to any third party. The only transfer of data outside your device is described in sections 2 and 4 above: to your own Google Drive, and to Google's Gemini API for lesson generation. No human at Compa has access to your Google account, your Drive, or your learning profile.

6. Where your data is stored, and for how long

Your learning data lives in two places, both under your control:

  • Your browser's local storage, so that Compa works offline. You can clear this at any time by clearing your browser's site data for Compa.
  • Your Google Drive, as the single file compa_profile.json, which you own and can open, copy, move, or delete like any other file.

Compa has no retention policy of its own because Compa holds no data. Your learning profile exists for as long as you choose to keep the file in your Drive.

7. Deleting your data and revoking access

  • To delete your learning profile, delete compa_profile.json from your Google Drive and clear Compa's site data in your browser. This removes all data the Application has ever stored.
  • To revoke Compa's access to your Google account at any time, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Compa. Compa will no longer be able to read or write the profile file.

8. Children's privacy

Compa is intended for learners of all ages, including school-age children, used with the knowledge of a parent, guardian, teacher, or school. Compa itself collects no information from any user, child or adult. All learning data resides in the Google account under which the learner signs in. Where that account is managed by a parent (through Family Link) or by a school (through Google Workspace for Education), the account manager retains full control over the data, including the ability to view or delete the profile file and to revoke Compa's access.

9. Compliance with the Google API Services User Data Policy

Compa's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new "last updated" date. Because Compa collects no contact information, we cannot notify you directly; please check this page from time to time.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer at info@entropraxis.org.
